Privacy policy

What happens to the details you send through the “Request a call with Andy” form on this website.

1. Who is responsible

Jacomy Mayne Studio, a design studio based in Mendoza, Argentina.

  • Email: studio@jacomymayne.com

2. What we ask for

  • Your name and email (required).
  • Your brand or company, a phone number and a line about your project (optional).
  • What goes in the bottle: spirits, wine, beer or other.
  • The parameters of the link or ad that brought you here (for example utm_source), so we know where enquiries come from.

Please don’t put sensitive personal information in the free-text line.

3. What we use it for

To read your enquiry and reply to you. We use the link parameters only to see which ad or link brought the enquiry. We don’t sell your details. By sending the form you agree to this use.

4. Who receives it

Roy Mayne and Andy Jacomy, at the studio. The form delivers your details to studio@jacomymayne.com and through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-delivery service that passes the message to our inbox and may process it outside Argentina. We use it only to deliver your enquiry to us.

We don’t share your details with anyone else unless the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

For 24 months after our last contact, and then we delete it. If we end up working together, we also keep what the contract and tax rules require.

6. Your rights

Under Argentina’s Personal Data Protection Law (Law 25,326) you can ask us to:

  • show you the details we hold about you (access);
  • correct or update them (rectification);
  • delete them (suppression).

Write to studio@jacomymayne.com from the address you used in the form. Access is free of charge, once every six months unless you show a legitimate interest. We will answer within the legal deadlines.

7. Cookies and measurement

Measurement and advertising tools load only if you click Accept in the banner. In use today: none. If we add any, we will list them here, with what each one is for.

They learn that a form was sent, never what you wrote in it, and the form is masked in session recordings. If you decline, the page and the form work the same.

8. If something goes wrong

You can complain to Argentina’s data protection authority, the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública (AAIP), which is the control body of Law 25,326.

La AGENCIA DE ACCESO A LA INFORMACIÓN PÚBLICA, en su carácter de Órgano de Control de la Ley N° 25.326, tiene la atribución de atender las denuncias y reclamos que interpongan quienes resulten afectados en sus derechos por incumplimiento de las normas vigentes en materia de protección de datos personales.
